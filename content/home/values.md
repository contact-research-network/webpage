We want a plural research agenda on intergroup contact, open to dissent, new ideas, and different methods. That needs equitable collaboration between researchers of different ethnic, national, linguistic, and religious backgrounds, gender and sexual identities, career stages, resources, and regions. Our membership does not yet reflect that range, and widening it is a priority. We also want to support a new generation of scholars whose work informs policy and promotes social inclusion.
