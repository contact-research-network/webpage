Are you interested in intergroup relations, perceptions of threat, political attitudes, and polarization? Looking to pursue a PhD at the intersection of social psychology and political science?
  The Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB) is recruiting a PhD candidate for an interdisciplinary research project exploring the psychological and contextual factors underlying support for far-right politics.
  
  **Research focus**
  - Perceptions of threat and authoritarian attitudes
  - Intergroup relations, prejudice, and political polarization
  - Support for far-right parties and candidates
  - The role of social and political contexts, with a particular focus on Brussels and Belgium
  
  📍 Location: CESCUP, Université libre de Bruxelles, Belgium
  
   📅 Application deadline: 6 November 2026 (position open until filled)
  
   💼 Duration: 3.5 years, full-time, paid PhD position
  
   🎓 Start date: 1 January 2027
  
  💰 Funding: Full-time scholarship comparable to a competitive Belgian salary, including healthcare and social security benefits
  
  **Who should apply?**
  Candidates with a Master's degree in social psychology or a related field, a strong interest in political attitudes and intergroup relations, and solid quantitative research and statistical skills. Excellent English and a collaborative mindset are essential.
  
  📩 How to apply: Send your CV, a detailed overview of your study results and grades, a motivation letter, and the names of two referees to jasper.van.assche@ulb.be.
  
  ✉️ Email subject: PhD position ARC avancé
  
  🔗 Learn more about the research group: https://cescup.ulb.be/
