Following our logo competition, ICRN has a new logo. Participants at the ICRN 2026 Summit in July rated four shortlisted designs, and the people mosaic was the clear favourite: 24 of 25 respondents said they liked or loved it.
  
  The mosaic fits together figures and shapes in many colours, with no piece standing above the others. It reflects what the network is about: researchers from different backgrounds, countries and career stages working together on equal terms.
  
  You will see the new logo across our website, newsletter and social media from today. Thank you to all who submitted ideas and took part in the vote.
