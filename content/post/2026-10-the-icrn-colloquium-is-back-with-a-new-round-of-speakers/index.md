We are excited to announce a brand-new round of the ICRN Colloquium, with a new line-up of wonderful speakers!
  
  As in previous years, colloquia take place on the last Monday or Friday of the month from October to May, with a winter break in December, typically 12-1 pm GMT.
  
  We are still finalising a few details before releasing the full list of speakers and schedule. In the meantime, we are delighted to announce that [Dr Nils Reimer](https://psych.ucsb.edu/people/faculty/nils-karl-reimer), University of California, Santa Barbara (USA), will kick us off on **Friday, 30 October 2026**: 4-5 pm GMT | 5-6 pm CET | 8-9 am PDT | 3-4 am (31 October) AEDT.
  
  Over the next few weeks, members will receive MS Teams invites to all ICRN colloquia, which should be added directly to your calendar in your own time zone. Please note that some talks may be recorded and some may not, depending on the speakers’ wishes. If you would prefer not to receive these invites, or have any questions, please let us know by replying to the member email.
  
  The schedule will be updated on the Colloquium page as more details become available. We look forward to seeing many of you there!
